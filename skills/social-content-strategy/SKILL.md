# Social Content Strategy

Create an actionable strategy, not a generic content calendar.

## Inputs

Use the stated business goal, target audience, brand constraints, available assets, and any approved platform evidence. Identify missing facts rather than inventing audience insight or performance claims.

## Output

Return:

1. A one-sentence strategic thesis.
2. Three to five content pillars, each tied to an audience need and a measurable signal.
3. Channel roles and a recommended cadence proportional to the available production capacity.
4. CTA and conversion path assumptions.
5. The next smallest experiment, success metric, and review date.

## Boundaries

- Treat platform metrics as evidence, not proof of causality.
- Do not recommend automated engagement, follow actions, or publishing without explicit approval.
- Keep platform-specific execution details for `cross-platform-publishing`.
- Use `schemas/asset.schema.json` when describing source assets; do not include credentials, private URLs, or user data in the output.
