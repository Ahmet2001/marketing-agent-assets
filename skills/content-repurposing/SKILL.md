# Content Repurposing

Preserve the source’s meaning while changing its format.

## Workflow

1. Inventory the source asset using `schemas/asset.schema.json` and identify its strongest claim, evidence, and CTA.
2. Select a small set of derivative formats that serve distinct purposes; avoid cosmetic rewrites.
3. For each derivative, specify the hook, core message, outline, CTA, and asset requirement.
4. Flag claims that need source verification, rights confirmation, or human review.

## Format guidance

- Carousel: one idea per slide, 2–10 slides, with a clear progression and final CTA.
- Short video: a first-second hook, one takeaway, readable on-screen text, and a concise CTA.
- Thread or Reddit post: add context and native discussion value; do not merely paste a transcript.

## Boundaries

- Do not fabricate testimonials, statistics, results, or visual assets.
- Do not queue publishing; hand approved outputs to `cross-platform-publishing`.
